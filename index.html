Your gauge

The values below are examples. Replace them with your own: measure the swatch after washing and drying, in the middle, away from the edges.

If you measured the swatch after washing, leave 0. If you measured it before washing, enter how much the piece will shrink (cotton usually 3–5%). If the yarn stretches (viscose, bamboo), enter a negative number, for example −4.

My gauges

Save a gauge under the name of a yarn, a pattern or a part of the piece (for example “1×1 ribbing”), then pick the one you need in the “Calculate with gauge” list. This lets you calculate different parts of one project from different gauges.

How to spread the change evenly

“Stitches at a time” is how many stitches are decreased or increased in one row: 2 means one at each edge, 1 means at one edge only. If shaping in the round happens in several places, enter their total.

For example, a row has 90 stitches and you need to add 12: the page shows after how many stitches to make each increase so they are spread evenly. An increase is made after a group of stitches; a decrease is two stitches worked together. If you do not want to change the edges, start the row with half of the first group and work the other half at the end of the row.

For the counter, the number of stitches at the end of each row is copied. Paste it into the “Row pattern” section of the “Row and stitch counter” page: data is not passed between pages automatically, because each one keeps it in its own browser storage.

Transfer and backup

Press “Copy data”, send the text to yourself (to notes or a messenger) and paste it into this field on another device.

Values are saved automatically in this browser on this device. The calculation is approximate: the density of your work may vary in different parts of the piece, so after the first 10 cm check the width and recalculate if needed.
